Evidence-to-report checklist

  1. Keep the original file without editing it.
  2. Save the URL/profile/message where it appeared.
  3. Record date and time and take relevant screenshots.
  4. Keep copies in a secure location.
  5. Use verified institutional/legal channels for reporting.
